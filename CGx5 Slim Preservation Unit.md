# Overview
[[Cryogenerics]]'s most recent experimental model, the CGx5 Slim Preservation Unit, seeks to improve upon the general form factor of all-in-one cryonics hardware without compromising on performance — subject matter "experts" claimed it could not be done, the CGx5 disagrees.

With careful rearrangement of the vitrification modules and a major *reduction* in the capacity of the built-in power-supply and batteries, a third of the overall width has been slimmed off the former model. In terms of accessibility, vessels that in the past were incapable of installing a cryogenic unit due to form factor now have a feasible option.

Still in an iterative prototyping phase, the CGx5 Slim Preservation Unit has been fitted into several hubs within Soteira and her sister ships for testing.

# Rulings
>*The CGx5 is a hardwired cryogenic unit heavily reliant on an external power source. This makes it increasingly susceptible to power surges and other electrical anomalies other cryogenic units would usually not have problems with.* 

On an **Electrical Anomaly**, encapsulated target and CGx5 Unit suffer 1d10 \[-] ( `dice:2d10kl`) Fire and Explosives DMG and target will begin thawing. 
If power is not restored or the unit would have suffered sufficient DMG to break it, whichever first, the unit deactivates and limps, crashing down into its horizontal wake-position. Provided the target is still alive, it will fully thaw in under 10 minutes.
