## Step 1 - *Roll Stats*
---
> Characters have four basic stats: 
> **Strength**, **Speed**, **Intellect**, and **Combat**.
> These stats reflect someone’s capacity to act under *extreme pressure*.

**Roll 2d10, add them together, then add 25.** — ( `dice:2d10 + 25`)
*Record the results for each Stat.*

## Step 2 - *Roll Saves*
---
>Characters have three *saves*:
>**Sanity**, **Fear**, and **Body**.
>Saves represent how resistant someone is to various forms of trauma and violence.

**Roll 2d10, add them together, then add 10.** — ( `dice:2d10 + 10`)
*Record the results for each Save.*

## Step 3 - *Choose a Class*
---
>Characters must pick between three broad classes. 
>**Combatant**, **Scholar**, or **Grubber**.
>Regardless of what position the character finds themselves in at the present moment, their class represents more a background; a life of experience. This experience influences acquired knowledge and traits related to an adjacent line of work that character may have undertook in the past.
##### Combatant %%(Marine)%%
---
>Soldiers, private security, and mercenary militia. Effective in a fight, but in a panic Combatants often pose a risk to companions.

**\+ 10 Combat**
**\+ 10 Body Save**
**\+ 20 Fear Save**
**\+ 1 Max Wounds**
##### Scholar %%(Scientist)%%
---
>Doctors, researchers, or anyone with a highly practised skills; often through academic training. Generally quite rational, exceptional in their chosen field.

**\+ 15 Intellect**
**\- 5 to Any One(1) Stat**
**\+ 30 Sanity Save**
##### Grubber %%(Teamster)%%
---
>The worn working-class of the Galactic Cluster. Calloused engineers, miners, pilots, technicians, sanitisers, and other essential land or space positions. All-round tough workers, seldom wavered.

**\+ 5 to All Stats**
**\+ 10 to All Saves**

## Step 4 - *Roll Health*
---
>You start at maximum health with zero wounds. Whenever your health falls below zero, you suffer a wound. Reset your health to its maximum, minus any carryover damage. When your wounds reach zero, you die.

**Roll 1d10, then add 10.** — ( `dice:1d10 + 10`)
*Record this value in both your current and maximum health box, and set your current wounds to 0 and maximum wounds to 2. Adjust maximum wounds with respect to your class.*

## Step 5 - Set Stress
---
>Everyone has at least one nagging thought. Unless you’re dead.

**Set your current and minimum stress to 2.**

## Step 6 - *Note Your Trauma Response*
---
**Combatant:** Whenever you panic, every close friendly player must make a *Fear Save*.
**Scholar:** Whenever you fail a *Sanity Save*, all close friendly players gain 1 *Stress*.
**Grubber:** Once per session, you may take advantage on a *Panic Check*.
