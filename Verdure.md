# Overview
Verdure is a bio-tech company with varying focuses including extreme botany, parasite research, human-transplanting, and trans-humanist technology. The company is on the verge of a breakthrough into [[Shells]], manufactured bodies Verdure hopes consciousness can be imprinted into.
