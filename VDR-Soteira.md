# Overview
The VDR-Soteira is [[Verdure]]’s elusive flagship research vessel. A phantom that the few far-space veterans tell drunken stories about. The Soteira can spend decades at a time skirting the edges of the [[far-space]], periodically stopping where necessary, and very rarely turning up anywhere near a core system.

Many of Verdure’s cutting edge projects are in active development aboard Soteira, most notably [[Project Rebirth]] reaching a climactic breakthrough. Due to the sensitivity of the undertakings aboard the vessel, competitors and opportunists alike thirsty for knowledge or a hefty payday have spent a considerable sum of resources attempting to hunt down and commandeer this prize beast
