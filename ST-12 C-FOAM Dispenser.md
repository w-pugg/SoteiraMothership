# Overview
> “*The [[Stitch’d]] ST-12 C-FOAM Dispenser is a specialty tool excelling at quickly and effectively filling holes in non-watertight volumes. Auxiliary usage includes but is not limited to: Applying weigh and mass to a target object, Cementing a target object in a particular location, and Creating structural volumes. Do not aim the operational end of the device toward any living being.*”

# Rulings
Shoots globs of carbon-infused foam, expanding rapidly and solidifying within seconds upon contact.
Each glob weighs approximately 3 kilograms, and forms spherical volumes roughly 15cm in diameter at full expansion.
A target struck with a C-FOAM glob must make a **Body Save \[-]** or become pinned. *(**Strength Check \[-]** to escape).*
Escaping deals 1d5 (`dice: 1d5`) [[Bleeding]] damage. 
Once hardened, a single glob of C-FOAM has the capacity to withstand 70 kilograms of weight.
