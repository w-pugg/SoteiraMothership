The product of [[Project Rebirth]], successful Shells are manufactured bodies capable of having a mapped consciousness imprinted into its psyche. These bodies are totally blank slates and can theoretically be designed to fit any particular image. As research continues however, it has been noted that an insignificant portion of bodies reveal ”undesirable artefacts” upon further testing. Leading researchers haven’t been able to pinpoint the root cause of these artefacts as they manifest in variable formats. The following have been identified as the most common.
