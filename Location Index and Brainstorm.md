Hymenium — *The hymenium is the fertile, spore-producing tissue layer on the fruiting body of fungi, commonly known as the "spore factory". Composed of asci (in Ascomycota) or basidia (in Basidiomycota), this layer matures spores for reproduction. It is often found on gills, inside pores, or on the surface of cups and spines.*
