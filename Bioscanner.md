>A light, handheld device with an oscillating fold-out scanner. A small radar display suspended from the main body of the scanner illustrates the positions of life signs within the scanned radius. 

While in-hand and operating, the Bioscanner pings the approximate locations of bio-signatures within a 15 square radius and outputs the results to a display every round.

Consumes a battery every 6 hours of use.
