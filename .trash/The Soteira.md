The **Soteira** *(named after the Greek feminine epithet meaning "Saviour", "Deliverer", or "Preserver")* is a colossal generation-class vessel owned by the [[Peregrine Group]]. The vessel was initially commissioned alongside two others (**Ourgos** and **Agrotera**) to conduct classified large-scale long-term scientific research tasks deep in the [[far-space]] focusing on exotic extraterrestrial lifeforms. The venture was greenlit with calculated hopes that any technological advancements would reciprocate competitive commercial gains and improve shareholder value.

As much of a financial gargantuan [[Peregrine Group]] may be, several decades of service with incredibly little to show for it — any revelations were either profitably irrelevant, or far too dangerous to put to market — forced the administrative wing responsible for Soteira and its sister ships to begrudgingly lease large segments of each ship to its corporate allies to begin recouping losses. This transaction meant the research departments aboard each ship at the time would be required to downsize to fit in their dramatically smaller boxes. However, new, corporately distinct research teams would go on to fill the vacancy in exchange for liquid assets and research contracts.

As the years passed, the companies now associated with Soteira and her sister ships would come to see the value of an unpoliced, relatively-off-the-books testing grounds skirting the edges of the far-space. The sorts of ethically questionable things you couldn’t get away with even on an X-Class planet could be realised on **Soteira** and that was an exciting prospect for the pragmatic scholar restricted by ideals. As long as invoices were paid in full, conflict between competing divisions remained off-ship, [[Peregrine Group]] was not concerned about what happens aboard its vessels.
