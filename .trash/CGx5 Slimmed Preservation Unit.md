# Overview
[[Cryogenerics]]'s most recent experimental model, the CGx5 PsvU, seeks to improve upon the general form factor of all-in-one cryonics hardware — subject matter "experts" claimed it could not be done.

With some careful rearrangement of the vitrification modules and a major reduction in the capacity of the built-in power supply, a significant third of the overall width has been slimmed off the former model. Compacting to this degree dramatically improves the accessibility and feasibility of personal cryonics hardware available to vessels that would otherwise be incapable.
