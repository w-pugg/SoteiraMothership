The question of what it means to ***be*** human has entranced the race's greatest minds for its entire existence, many postulating the truth is written amongst the stars. Conclusively, upon touching the many great stars stretching out into the expanse beyond the humble skies of earth, our birthright of foolishness has, time and again, led us to an answer that lies in a cold, lonely reality.  

___  

Many crave praise, for the lengths we've come as a species, from those who created us and our ancestors. A collegial welcoming from a league of extraterrestrial beings. Beings who have neglected us to forge our *own* wings and join this intergalactic society who would now see us as worthy.
