# Overview
Rachel Kilroy has recently claimed the position of Commander of [[AntiSEC]] after the former commander was slain by a mutinous associated mercenary. Her most recent orders are to stage an attack on the phantom [[VDR-Soteira]] instructing AntiSEC forces to scrape as much proprietary data as possible and compile it for the [[Anonymous Competing Corporation|client]]. Additionally, she has also been instructed by a separate [[interested party]] to verify particular claims that [[Verdure]] has successfully completed a human-[[Shells|shell]] transfer.
