# Overview
Rachel Kilroy has recently assumed the position of Commander of the Troubleshooters after the former commander was slain by a mutinous associated mercenary. Her most recent orders are to stage an attack on the [[VDR-Soteira]] with the primary directive being to verify the claims that [[Verdure]] has successfully completed a human-[[Shells|shell]] transfer. Provided the claims are true, her secondary directive is to scrape as much data and physical evidence as possible and return it to [[Anonymous Competing Corporation]].
