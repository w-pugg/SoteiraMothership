# Overview
An experimental prototype designed by [[Yamaha Corp.]]. A shoulder-mounted, cumbersome hunk of engineering with a collinear handlebar on the top edge and a 45° left-handed fore-grip positioned nearer the barrel end. the K780D-4YM Disassembler was initially designed for commercial excavation purposes and has been modified for personnel usage. It would be reasonable to assume a weapon of this caliber would be illegal almost everywhere.

# Rulings
Fires a beam that causes matter to break apart on the molecular level.
Requires two rounds to cooldown enough to fire again without damaging the barrel risking **Overheat:** cumulative 1-in-10 chance of exploding (deals 2d10 ( `dice:2d10`) **Fire and Explosives** DMG to shooter & all **Adjacent**). 
Consumes four batteries per shot.
