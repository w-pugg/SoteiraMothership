# Overview
>An experimental prototype designed by [[Yamaha Corp.]]. A shoulder-mounted, cumbersome hunk of engineering with a collinear handlebar on the top edge and a 45° left-handed fore-grip positioned nearer the barrel end. the K780D-4YM Disassembler was initially designed for commercial excavation purposes and has been modified for personnel usage. It would be safe to assume a weapon of this calibre would be illegal almost everywhere.

---
# Special
Fires a beam that causes matter to break apart on the molecular level.
Requires two rounds to cooldown enough to fire again without damaging the barrel. 
Consumes four batteries per shot.
