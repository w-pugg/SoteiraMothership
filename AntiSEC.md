# Overview
AntiSEC are a paramilitary crab bucket of mercenaries with very little formal leadership. The organisation is currently headed by a [[Commander Rachel Kilroy]] and orders are generally carried out by a menagerie of minor commanding officers. 

With a contract from [[Anonymous Competing Corporation]], a majority of AntiSEC's forces are currently storming the [[VDR-Soteira]] attempting to steal corporate data.
