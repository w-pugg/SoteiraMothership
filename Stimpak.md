> A single-use, needle-administered, handheld medical device.

Cures Cryosickness.
Reduces Stress by 1.
Restores Health by 1d10 ( `dice:1d10`).
Grants [+] to all rolls for 1d10 ( `dice:1d10`) minutes.

After administration, roll 1d10 ( `dice:1d10`). If you roll under the number of doses you have taken in the past 24 hours, make a ***Death Save***.
