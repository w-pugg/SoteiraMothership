The standard Mothership ruleset handles range and movement abstractly in range bands. This document should elaborate upon these abstractions for a 3D grid-based mapping system. 

## Adjacent — *It Can Touch You*
---
*Within one square.*

You are basically touching. Adjacency is important for interacting with physical devices and other PCs, melee combat, and often cover. You can talk comfortably within this range, hear whispers, and feasibly smell things.

## Close Range — *It Can Get To You*
---
*One to five squares.*

You can quickly move to someone within this range in a few seconds. Firearms such as handguns and shotguns are most effective within close range. Communicating requires an outdoor voice and olfactory abilities reach their limits unless the scent is particularly potent.

## Long Range — *You Can Shoot It* 
---
*Six to twenty squares.*

At long range, things often take a round or longer to reach. Rifles are effective at this range and the efficacy of shotguns begins to wane. You would need to yell to get someone’s attention, and you surely can’t smell them at this range.``

## Far Range — *You Can Hear Their Screams*
---
*Twenty-one to one-hundred squares.*

Most weapons become ineffective at far range. Only sniper rifles and dedicated armaments are capable of providing an accurate impact. Things at this range take several turns to reach, and communicating with someone becomes exponentially more impossible the further down this band they go.
