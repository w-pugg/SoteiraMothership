When gaining wounds *(through reducing your health to zero)*, you’ll suffer a detrimental and often persistent effect related to that wound until the wound recovers. Provided the wound is capable of recovery…

The types of wounds are:
- **Blunt Force**
	- Getting punched, bludgeoned by a bat, hit by a thrown object, or falling a significant distance.
- **Bleeding**
	- Getting stabbed or cut, often by a blade.
- **Gunshot**
	- Being shot by a firearm, or perforated by other projectile means.
- **Fire & Explosives**
	- Affected by fire, heat, radiation, electricity, or caustic agents.
- **Gore**
	- Gruesome attacks.
