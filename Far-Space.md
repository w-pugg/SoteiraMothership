An unfathomable emptiness spanning the gap between clusters of galaxies. As time progresses, the universe slowly expands, consequently increasing these spaces in between. Man-kind has not developed the required infrastructure to journey safely through Far-Space yet, with many of the brightest minds working on solutions. 

As far as we know, matter still exists within Far-Space albeit sparsely. Rogue asteroids and other space debris migrating from one cluster to another make up the bulk of this material. However, the longer Soteira spends skirting our inter-galactic pen, our reason to believe something disturbingly otherworldly lurks deep in the shadows grows ever clearer.
