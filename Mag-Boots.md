> Boots that grant the wearer the ability to easily walk on *Magnetic* surfaces despite gravitational forces (or the lack there of).

Can be toggled on or off.

While toggled on, the user may walk on *Magnetic* surfaces unimpeded by gravitational force, however the user’s movement is halved on *Magnetic* surfaces.
