Currently [[Verdure]]’s most promising research effort, Project Rebirth’s primary goal is to preserve the minds of today for the world of tomorrow. 

>Considered a broadly fundamental flaw of the human condition is its impermanence. Our minds and bodies learn and grow through decades of experience to simply wither and decay. We are expected to accept this fact unquestioningly and move on with our lives. **The many here at Verdure disagree.** 

Verdure seems to have made a critical breakthrough in the realm of human consciousness. Leaks claim that the tech giant has been able to synthesise a human body they call a [[Shells|shell]] and artificially imprint the body with an external, pre-existing consciousness. If these claims are true, it would be reasonable to assume "immortality" is a very real possibility.
